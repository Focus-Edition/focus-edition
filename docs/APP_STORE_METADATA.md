# Focus Edition — App Store & Google Play Metadata

## App Name & Subtitle
- **App Name:** Focus Edition — ADHD Document Reader
- **Subtitle:** Turn dense docs into 4-min missions
- **Category:** Productivity / Education / Accessibility
- **Target Audience:** ADHD, AuDHD, Dyslexia, and anyone struggling with overwhelming statutory, corporate, or academic documents.

## Promotional Text
Stop dreading dense documents. Focus Edition breaks complex factsheets, handbooks, contracts, and letters into short, source-grounded missions with active recall quizzes, bionic reading, and zero artificial content.

## Full Description
Focus Edition turns long, overwhelming documents into short, completable missions designed specifically for ADHD brains.

### Key Features
1. **Real Document Ingestion Pipeline:**
   - Ingest TXT, Markdown, HTML, native PDF, scanned PDF, DOCX, and camera photos.
   - Built-in on-device OCR for scanned booklets and receipts.
   - Zero invented text: every mission, quiz, and card is 100% traceable to original source text.

2. **Source Grounding & Evidence:**
   - Every mission displays its exact source locator (page number, section heading, character range).
   - "Show Source" button on every mission, quiz question, and flashcard reveals the exact verbatim text snippet.

3. **Cognitive Accessibility & Neurodivergent Focus:**
   - **Bionic Reading Mode:** Guides eye fixation with adjustable bolding and opacity.
   - **Dyslexia-Friendly Typography:** OpenDyslexic, Lexend, Atkinson Hyperlegible, and Inter.
   - **Reading Ruler:** Movable visual focus bar preventing line skipping.
   - **Focus Mode:** One-tap distraction-free view hiding all tabs and notifications.
   - **Integrated Focus Timers:** 15m, 25m, 35m, 50m intervals with gentle break reminders.
   - **Audio Reading (TTS):** Toggle speech aloud with instant stop on second tap.

4. **Strict Document Privacy:**
   - Your private documents are never sent to external AI services unless you explicitly enable that option in settings.
   - Private document storage with user-isolated encryption.
   - Full offline support with automatic synchronization when connected.

5. **Active Recall Learning:**
   - Unique factual quizzes and flashcards derived directly from statutory numbers, caps, definitions, and rules.

## Privacy & Permissions
- **Camera (NSCameraUsageDescription):** Focus Edition uses the camera to photograph physical documents, booklets, and notes for on-device OCR and mission generation.
- **Photo Library (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription):** Focus Edition accesses photos to import images of documents and notes for OCR and mission generation.
- **Speech Recognition (NSSpeechRecognitionUsageDescription):** Focus Edition uses speech features for text-to-speech audio reading.
- **Data Collection:** User documents are stored privately in encrypted user storage. No document data is shared with third parties or AI models without explicit opt-in.
